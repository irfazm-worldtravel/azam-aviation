Makkah & Madinah Travel Routes

Pilgrims can plan their journey around flight arrival points, hotel locations and preferred transport. The following are common route options; the best sequence depends on your flights and travel arrangements.

ROUTE 01 · ARRIVE IN JEDDAH

Jeddah Airport → Makkah → Madinah

Arrive at King Abdulaziz International Airport in Jeddah, continue to Makkah by pre-arranged road transfer or the Haramain High-Speed Railway, perform Umrah, and then travel to Madinah by train or road.

ROUTE 02 · ARRIVE IN MADINAH

Madinah → Makkah → Jeddah

Arrive in Madinah, visit Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, and then travel to Makkah by Haramain train or road. Those intending Umrah must observe the applicable Miqat and Ihram requirements before proceeding to Makkah.

BETWEEN THE TWO HOLY CITIES

Makkah ↔ Madinah

The Haramain High-Speed Railway connects Makkah and Madinah through Jeddah and King Abdulaziz International Airport. Private cars, buses and other road transfers may also be available. Check current schedules, fares and luggage rules before travel.

LOCAL TRANSFERS

Airport, hotel & Haram transfers

Plan transfers between the airport or railway station, your hotel and the Holy Mosques. Allow extra time during Ramadan, Hajj season, Fridays and other busy periods, and confirm vehicle access and drop-off points in advance.

Important Ihram reminder: Pilgrims travelling to Makkah intending to perform Umrah or Hajj must enter Ihram at the correct Miqat or when passing its alignment. Air travellers should prepare before the aircraft reaches the Miqat. If you are unsure which Miqat applies to your route, seek qualified guidance before departure.
Explore the holy city

Makkah Ziyarah & Places of Interest

Visitors may wish to learn about these locations during their stay in Makkah. Visits are optional and should be planned respectfully, according to local access rules and current conditions.

Masjid al-Haram & The Holy Kaaba

The central destination for Umrah and Hajj, where pilgrims perform the prescribed rites. Follow official crowd-management instructions and mosque guidance.

Jabal al-Noor & Cave of Hira

A mountain associated with the first revelation. The climb is strenuous; visitors should assess their fitness, use suitable footwear and avoid attempting it in extreme heat.

Jabal Thawr

A mountain associated with the Hijrah journey. The ascent is challenging and is not suitable for everyone; viewing the area from a safe location may be preferable.

Mina

A major Hajj site where pilgrims stay during designated days of Hajj and perform the stoning rites at Jamarat according to the Hajj schedule.

Arafat & Jabal al-Rahmah

The plain of Arafat is central to Hajj. Standing at Arafat on the prescribed day is a fundamental pillar of Hajj; outside Hajj, visitors should respect site access and local guidance.

Muzdalifah

An important Hajj location where pilgrims stay after leaving Arafat on the prescribed night, following the official Hajj arrangements.

Visit the city of the Prophet ﷺ

Madinah Ziyarah & Places of Interest

Madinah offers visitors an opportunity to pray at the Prophet’s Mosque and learn about important places in Islamic history.

Al-Masjid an-Nabawi

The Prophet’s Mosque is the principal destination for visitors to Madinah. Maintain quiet, follow mosque instructions and show consideration for other worshippers.

Al-Rawdah Al-Sharifah

Visitors should check the current Nusuk app process and permit requirements before planning a visit. Entry arrangements and availability can change.

Quba Mosque

A historic mosque in Madinah with special significance in Islamic history. Visitors can check local access and prayer arrangements before travelling.

Masjid al-Qiblatain

A well-known historic mosque associated with the change of the direction of prayer. Visitors should follow the site's current visitor guidance.

Mount Uhud & Uhud Martyrs Cemetery

An important historical area connected with the Battle of Uhud. Visitors are encouraged to behave respectfully and follow local instructions.

Al-Baqi Cemetery

The historic cemetery near the Prophet’s Mosque. Access arrangements and visiting times are subject to local rules and may change.

Before you travel

Useful Umrah & Hajj Travel Information

Documents & official requirements

  • Check passport validity, visa conditions and current Saudi entry requirements.
  • Confirm the applicable Umrah or Hajj permits and approved booking process.
  • Keep copies of travel documents, hotel details and emergency contacts.

Nusuk app & Rawdah visits

  • Use official Nusuk services for applicable permits, bookings and updated guidance.
  • Check Rawdah appointment rules and availability before visiting.
  • Follow official instructions at the Holy Mosques and other sites.

Health, comfort & safety

  • Wear comfortable footwear and stay hydrated.
  • Carry prescribed medicines and follow health advice from your clinician.
  • Keep your group together and agree on a meeting point in crowded areas.
  • Allow rest time, especially for elderly travellers and children.

Hajj planning

  • Hajj has specific dates, rites, permits and official arrangements.
  • Follow instructions from the authorised Hajj provider and Saudi authorities.
  • Do not travel for Hajj on an assumption that an ordinary travel visa permits Hajj participation.
Please note: Ziyarah visits are educational and optional; they are not additional required rites of Umrah. Hajj rites, permits, access rules, transport schedules and visa conditions are subject to official Saudi regulations and may change. Always verify the latest instructions before travel.

Official information: Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah · Nusuk · Haramain Railway
