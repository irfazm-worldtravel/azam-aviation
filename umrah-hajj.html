Plan your journey

Makkah & Madinah Travel Routes

Pilgrims can plan their journey around flight arrival points, hotel locations and preferred transport. The following are common route options; the best sequence depends on your flights and travel arrangements.

ROUTE 01 · ARRIVE IN JEDDAH

Jeddah Airport → Makkah → Madinah

Arrive at King Abdulaziz International Airport in Jeddah, continue to Makkah by pre-arranged road transfer or the Haramain High-Speed Railway, perform Umrah, and then travel to Madinah by train or road.

ROUTE 02 · ARRIVE IN MADINAH

Madinah → Makkah → Jeddah

Arrive in Madinah, visit Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, and then travel to Makkah by Haramain train or road. Those intending Umrah must observe the applicable Miqat and Ihram requirements before proceeding to Makkah.

BETWEEN THE TWO HOLY CITIES

Makkah ↔ Madinah

The Haramain High-Speed Railway connects Makkah and Madinah through Jeddah and King Abdulaziz International Airport. Private cars, buses and other road transfers may also be available. Check current schedules, fares and luggage rules before travel.

LOCAL TRANSFERS

Airport, hotel & Haram transfers

Plan transfers between the airport or railway station, your hotel and the Holy Mosques. Allow extra time during Ramadan, Hajj season, Fridays and other busy periods, and confirm vehicle access and drop-off points in advance.

Important Ihram reminder: Pilgrims travelling to Makkah intending to perform Umrah or Hajj must enter Ihram at the correct Miqat or when passing its alignment. Air travellers should prepare before the aircraft reaches the Miqat. If you are unsure which Miqat applies to your route, seek qualified guidance before departure.
Explore the holy city

Makkah Ziyarah & Places of Interest

Visitors may wish to learn about these locations during their stay in Makkah. Visits are optional and should be planned respectfully, according to local access rules and current conditions.

Masjid al-Haram & The Holy Kaaba

The central destination for Umrah and Hajj, where pilgrims perform the prescribed rites. Follow official crowd-management instructions and mosque guidance.

Jabal al-Noor & Cave of Hira

A mountain associated with the first revelation. The climb is strenuous; visitors should assess their fitness, use suitable footwear and avoid attempting it in extreme heat.

Jabal Thawr

A mountain associated with the Hijrah journey. The ascent is challenging and is not suitable for everyone; viewing the area from a safe location may be preferable.

Mina

A major Hajj site where pilgrims stay during designated days of Hajj and perform the stoning rites at Jamarat according to the Hajj schedule.

Arafat & Jabal al-Rahmah

The plain of Arafat is central to Hajj. Standing at Arafat on the prescribed day is a fundamental pillar of Hajj; outside Hajj, visitors should respect site access and local guidance.

Muzdalifah

An important Hajj location where pilgrims stay after leaving Arafat on the prescribed night, following the official Hajj arrangements.

Visit the city of the Prophet ﷺ

Madinah Ziyarah & Places of Interest

Madinah offers visitors an opportunity to pray at the Prophet’s Mosque and learn about important places in Islamic history.

Al-Masjid an-Nabawi

The Prophet’s Mosque is the principal destination for visitors to Madinah. Maintain quiet, follow mosque instructions and show consideration for other worshippers.

Al-Rawdah Al-Sharifah

Visitors should check the current Nusuk app process and permit requirements before planning a visit. Entry arrangements and availability can change.

Quba Mosque

A historic mosque in Madinah with special significance in Islamic history. Visitors can check local access and prayer arrangements before travelling.

Masjid al-Qiblatain

A well-known historic mosque associated with the change of the direction of prayer. Visitors should follow the site's current visitor guidance.

Mount Uhud & Uhud Martyrs Cemetery

An important historical area connected with the Battle of Uhud. Visitors are encouraged to behave respectfully and follow local instructions.

Al-Baqi Cemetery

The historic cemetery near the Prophet’s Mosque. Access arrangements and visiting times are subject to local rules and may change.

Before you travel

Useful Umrah & Hajj Travel Information

Documents & official requirements

  • Check passport validity, visa conditions and current Saudi entry requirements.
  • Confirm the applicable Umrah or Hajj permits and approved booking process.
  • Keep copies of travel documents, hotel details and emergency contacts.

Nusuk app & Rawdah visits

  • Use official Nusuk services for applicable permits, bookings and updated guidance.
  • Check Rawdah appointment rules and availability before visiting.
  • Follow official instructions at the Holy Mosques and other sites.

Health, comfort & safety

  • Wear comfortable footwear and stay hydrated.
  • Carry prescribed medicines and follow health advice from your clinician.
  • Keep your group together and agree on a meeting point in crowded areas.
  • Allow rest time, especially for elderly travellers and children.

Hajj planning

  • Hajj has specific dates, rites, permits and official arrangements.
  • Follow instructions from the authorised Hajj provider and Saudi authorities.
  • Do not travel for Hajj on an assumption that an ordinary travel visa permits Hajj participation.
Please note: Ziyarah visits are educational and optional; they are not additional required rites of Umrah. Hajj rites, permits, access rules, transport schedules and visa conditions are subject to official Saudi regulations and may change. Always verify the latest instructions before travel.

A simple guide for first-time pilgrims

Umrah: Main Steps Explained

Umrah can be performed at different times of the year, subject to current Saudi regulations. These are the main rites in a simple overview. For detailed rulings and differences of opinion, follow a qualified scholar or your authorised group guide.

STEP 01

Prepare for Ihram

Prepare before reaching the applicable Miqat. Make the intention for Umrah and begin the Talbiyah. Air travellers should plan ahead and not wait until after passing the Miqat.

STEP 02

Perform Tawaf

At Masjid al-Haram, perform seven circuits around the Kaaba, beginning at the Black Stone line, while following the official crowd-management instructions.

STEP 03

Complete Sa'i

Walk seven passages between Safa and Marwah, beginning at Safa and finishing at Marwah. Use the designated accessible routes where needed.

STEP 04

Hair trimming or shaving

After Sa'i, men shave or shorten the hair and women trim a small portion according to the applicable guidance. This completes the main rites of Umrah.

Helpful reminder: Keep your group contact details and hotel location with you. If you need wheelchair assistance or other support, arrange it through the relevant official service provider in advance.
Understand the pilgrimage days

Hajj: Key Places & Main Stages

Hajj takes place on specific dates in Dhul Hijjah and has detailed rites. The outline below is a general orientation, not a substitute for your authorised Hajj operator's schedule or qualified religious guidance.

8 DHUL HIJJAH · MINA

Journey to Mina

Pilgrims proceed to Mina according to their official group schedule and perform the prescribed prayers and worship there.

9 DHUL HIJJAH · ARAFAT

Day of Arafat

Pilgrims spend the designated time at Arafat in supplication and worship. After sunset, they proceed to Muzdalifah as directed by their Hajj provider.

NIGHT OF 9TH · MUZDALIFAH

Stay at Muzdalifah

Pilgrims follow their group's arrangements for the night and onward movement. Rest, safety and transport instructions are especially important in crowded conditions.

10 DHUL HIJJAH · EID DAY

Jamarat & completion rites

Pilgrims perform the prescribed rites, including stoning at Jamarat and the relevant sacrifice, hair cutting or shaving and Tawaf al-Ifadah, according to their Hajj type and authorised schedule.

11–13 DHUL HIJJAH

Days in Mina

Pilgrims complete the prescribed stoning rites on the appointed days and depart Mina in accordance with their Hajj plan and official instructions.

BEFORE DEPARTURE

Final arrangements

Confirm any remaining rites, hotel check-out, luggage collection and airport transfer with your group leader. Do not make independent changes to official transport arrangements.

Hajj is permit-controlled: Hajj participation requires the correct authorisation and official arrangements for the relevant season. An ordinary visit or Umrah visa should never be assumed to permit Hajj. Confirm eligibility and booking channels through the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah.
Travel between the holy cities

Haramain Train & Road Transfer Guide

The Haramain High-Speed Railway is a popular option for travel between Makkah, Jeddah and Madinah. It connects five main stations along a route of approximately 453 kilometres.

Haramain railway stations

  • Makkah Station
  • Jeddah Al-Sulaymaniyah Station
  • King Abdulaziz International Airport Station, Jeddah
  • King Abdullah Economic City Station
  • Madinah Station

Direct Makkah–Madinah journeys are approximately 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 20 minutes, depending on the service and timetable.

Private car or bus transfer

Road transfers can be useful for families, groups, passengers with extra luggage or travellers whose hotel locations are not close to railway stations. Journey time varies with traffic, season, route and checkpoints.

Confirm vehicle size, luggage capacity, pick-up point, drop-off point and total fare before departure.

Airport arrival in Jeddah

Passengers arriving at King Abdulaziz International Airport can check the current Haramain service from the airport station or arrange a pre-booked road transfer. Allow time for immigration, baggage collection and station access.

Station-to-hotel transfers

Railway stations are not necessarily beside your hotel or the Holy Mosques. Arrange a taxi, shuttle or private transfer for the final part of the journey, and confirm the correct hotel entrance and luggage arrangements.

Before booking: Train schedules, fares, luggage rules, station services and availability can change. Check the official Haramain Railway website for the latest details and book through its authorised channels.

Example 7-Day Umrah & Madinah Plan

This sample sequence can help with early planning. It is not a fixed package or a promise of availability; actual nights, transport and Ziyarah visits should be adjusted to flight timings, hotel location, health needs and current regulations.

DAYS 1–3

Madinah stay

Arrive, check in and rest. Visit Al-Masjid an-Nabawi for prayers, check current arrangements for Rawdah and consider a respectful Ziyarah programme to Quba Mosque, Masjid al-Qiblatain and the Uhud area.

DAY 4

Travel to Makkah

Travel by Haramain train or pre-arranged road transfer. Ensure Ihram and Miqat arrangements are correct for your route if you intend to perform Umrah on arrival.

DAYS 5–6

Makkah stay

Perform Umrah when ready, allow time for rest and prayers, and plan any optional local visits around your health, group arrangements and local access guidance.

DAY 7

Departure

Check out, confirm your airport transfer and allow sufficient time for the journey, check-in and airport procedures. Your actual departure may require an additional night or an earlier transfer.

For pilgrims departing Pakistan

Pre-Departure Checklist

Use this checklist when preparing for travel from Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad or another departure city. Requirements vary by traveller and season, so confirm the latest instructions before payment and departure.

Passport, visa & permits

  • Check passport validity against the current Saudi entry requirements.
  • Confirm the correct visa category and permitted length of stay.
  • Check current Umrah permit and Rawdah appointment procedures through official Nusuk services.
  • For Hajj, use only the authorised Hajj process and obtain the required Hajj permit.

Flights & accommodation

  • Confirm the arrival airport, return airport and flight timings.
  • Check hotel names, addresses, check-in dates and distance from the Haram.
  • Plan airport, railway-station and hotel transfers before departure.
  • Keep booking confirmations and emergency contacts accessible.

Health & personal preparation

  • Review current health and vaccination requirements for your travel dates.
  • Carry regular medicines in their original packaging with prescriptions where appropriate.
  • Choose comfortable footwear and prepare for walking and crowded areas.
  • Arrange mobility assistance for elderly passengers or anyone who needs it.

Money, communication & luggage

  • Plan access to payment methods and some Saudi Riyals for small expenses.
  • Arrange mobile roaming or a local SIM/eSIM after checking available options.
  • Label luggage and keep essential items in hand baggage.
  • Check airline baggage limits and Haramain train luggage rules separately.
Official updates: Visa conditions, permits, health rules and Hajj-season restrictions can change. Confirm the requirements for your own nationality, visa and travel dates with the relevant Saudi authorities and your authorised service provider.
Common questions

Umrah & Hajj Travel FAQs

Can I travel from Madinah to Makkah by train?

Yes. The Haramain High-Speed Railway connects Madinah and Makkah, with services also linking Jeddah and King Abdullah Economic City. Check the current timetable before booking.

Is Ziyarah compulsory for Umrah?

No. Visits to historical places in Makkah and Madinah are optional educational visits and are not additional required rites of Umrah.

Do I need a permit for Umrah?

Requirements and procedures can change. Check the latest instructions in the official Nusuk app and with the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah before travel.

Can an ordinary visa be used for Hajj?

Do not assume so. Hajj requires the correct Hajj authorisation and official arrangements for the season. Verify the current rules with Saudi authorities.

Can Azam Aviation arrange a complete package?

Contact our team with your travel dates, departure city, number of passengers and preferred hotel category. We will explain the services and availability that can be confirmed at the time of enquiry.

What should elderly pilgrims consider?

Plan realistic walking distances, rest breaks, accessible transport and any required assistance in advance. Keep essential medicines and emergency contact details accessible.
